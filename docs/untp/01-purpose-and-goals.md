# UNTP Purpose, Goals & Success

## Sources
- About: https://untp.unece.org/docs/about/
- Goals & Objectives: https://untp.unece.org/docs/about/Goals/

## Key points
- UNTP is a protocol, not a platform. It is intended to let independent technology platforms participate in interoperable supply chains.
- Its architecture covers product, facility, traceability, conformity and identity data.
- Each supply-chain actor can implement independently while retaining control of its data.
- UNTP is the practical application of UN/CEFACT Recommendation 49, adopted by the UN/CEFACT Plenary in July 2025.
- The current documentation states that public review has closed and that UNTP 1.0 is expected on 1 September 2026.
- The documentation currently labels the specification as suitable for pre-production pilots.
- Core goals include trustworthy sustainability claims, due diligence, a level playing field, improved market access, lower data-exchange cost and support for sustainable/circular value chains.
- Uptake is the primary success measure, with implementation and completed conformity tests used as direct indicators.

## Soulverse relevance
The strongest strategic implication is that Soulverse does not need to replace UNTP. A product that conforms to UNTP can become part of the interoperable implementation ecosystem.
