# Interoperability & Related Standards

## Source
- https://untp.unece.org/docs/about/References/

## Core principle
UNTP explicitly follows a "reuse rather than reinvent" strategy. It builds on established standards and attempts to maximise interoperability rather than creating a separate proprietary technology stack.

## Major standards referenced
- W3C Verifiable Credentials Data Model
- W3C Decentralised Identifiers
- W3C JSON-LD
- IETF JOSE
- IETF SD-JWT
- ISO Product Circularity Data Sheet
- CEN/CENELEC Digital Product Passport work
- ISO EPC Information Services
- Other industry, regulatory and UN/IGO initiatives

## Relationship to VCs and DIDs
UNTP uses W3C VCs for its core credential types and W3C DIDs for issuer identity. It also uses JSON-LD for semantic interoperability.

## Strategic implication
This validates the general architecture behind Soulverse's VC/DID direction, but it also means Soulverse's proprietary value should sit above or around the open standards rather than attempting to replace them.

## Potential differentiation
A Soulverse implementation could focus on:
- trust registry infrastructure;
- trust discovery;
- issuer/entity verification;
- government and enterprise integration;
- policy/governance tooling;
- UNTP conformance support;
- operational infrastructure for organisations adopting the standard.
