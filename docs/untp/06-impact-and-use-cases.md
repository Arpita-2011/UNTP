# Impact, Use Cases & Business Value

## Source
- https://untp.unece.org/docs/about/Impact/

## Main impact domains
UNTP positions verifiable supply-chain information as infrastructure for:
- anti-corruption;
- counterfeit detection and product authenticity;
- critical-minerals and strategic-material security;
- recall and incident response;
- sanctions/export-control compliance;
- supply-chain disruption and concentration-risk analysis;
- biodiversity;
- circular economy;
- human rights/welfare;
- product transparency;
- greenwashing reduction;
- sustainability reporting;
- verifiable ESG performance.

## Common technical mechanism
The recurring pattern is:
1. establish an identity;
2. issue signed credentials;
3. link credentials across the supply chain;
4. make the evidence discoverable;
5. verify status and integrity;
6. allow controlled disclosure of sensitive information.

## Business implication
The strongest commercial opportunity is not simply "VC issuance." It is enabling organisations to operate trusted, interoperable supply-chain evidence systems without forcing every participant onto the same platform.

## Soulverse opportunity
This supports a product proposition around trust infrastructure and implementation services for governments, enterprises and ecosystem operators adopting interoperable digital trust standards.
