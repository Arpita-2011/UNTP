# UNTP Audiences & Market Opportunity

## Source
- https://untp.unece.org/docs/0.7.0/about/Audience/

## Audience clusters
UNTP explicitly identifies:
- Primary producers and manufacturers
- Brands and retailers
- Regulators
- Financial institutions
- Accreditation and certification organisations
- ESG standards organisations
- Industry associations
- Environmental and human-welfare organisations
- Circular-economy participants
- Transport and logistics providers
- Consumers
- Software developers
- Service providers

## Particularly relevant to Soulverse
### Software developers
UNTP describes ERP, ESG-management and traceability systems as important implementation points. Developers can issue UNTP credentials and consume credentials discovered and verified through the protocol.

### Service providers
UNTP explicitly identifies implementation support and systems integration as a market opportunity. Large and medium-sized businesses are expected to need business analysis and integration support.

### Regulators
Regulators can issue verifiable identity and compliance credentials and use supply-chain data for automated compliance.

### Accreditation/certification
UNTP aims to make the chain of trust behind conformity certificates digitally verifiable.

## Strategic implication
Soulverse can potentially occupy both:
1. software/infrastructure implementer; and
2. implementation/integration partner.

This is stronger than positioning Soulverse merely as a VC/DID vendor.
