# FAQ, Governance & Participation

## Source
- https://untp.unece.org/docs/about/FAQ/

## Key points
- UNTP is free to use and can be modified for internal purposes.
- Formal registered extensions must follow UNTP extension methodology and governance rules.
- UNTP is positioned as an open, non-proprietary framework rather than a commercial platform.
- It aims to complement existing national, international and industry systems.
- The project seeks alignment with standards bodies such as ISO, ITU and GS1.
- Future updates and formal recommendations follow UN/CEFACT procedures with member-State engagement.
- Participation is open to governments, companies, researchers and civil society through code/specification contributions, testing, implementation and proposed extensions.
- A public registry tracks vendors and systems using UNTP.

## Strategic implication for Soulverse
There are three distinct participation routes:
1. implement the protocol;
2. contribute to the open-source/specification ecosystem;
3. propose or participate in extensions and governance.

These should be treated as separate strategic tracks.
